# Hex Hordes — reels, ready to post

Three vertical reels (1080×1920, 30 fps, ~20 s), cut from real battles in the game: the game's own
AI played both sides, every frame was rendered by the game, and the sound effects are the game's own,
in sync. Each reel comes in two versions:

- `ReelN….mp4`: royalty-free music baked in, every cut on its beat. Post as is (credit below).
- `ReelN…-game-sound.mp4`: the same edit with the game's sound only, cut to the same beat. Add a
  trending sound in Instagram's / TikTok's audio picker (suggestions below; check they're still
  trending, and line up the drop with the first cut).

Covers: `CoverN….jpg` (title in the middle band, so it survives the profile grid's crop).
